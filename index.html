Michal Sela "Safe Home" Hackathon — 1st Place

May 2024 · Israeli President's Award

The mission: Use technology to help women feel safe at home — in honor of Michal Sela, a victim of spousal abuse whose story ended in murder. Our goal was to detect the early, invisible signs of abuse before they escalate.

The business model: Since the Michal Sela Foundation is a non-profit, we designed a self-sustaining solution requiring no external funding. The app's front-end operated as an online shopping platform partnering with real vendors who support the cause — a percentage of every sale is donated to the foundation.

The technical solution: Behind the shopping interface, the app used OpenAI's Whisper automatic speech recognition model to passively transcribe audio in the background. The transcriptions were fed into a localized ML classification model trained on professional material about spousal abuse and data from online support forums. The pipeline flagged gray-area indicators of abuse — financial bullying, gaslighting, and non-physical patterns of control — enabling early detection and intervention before situations escalate to violence.

The panel: Judged in the presence of President Isaac Herzog, alongside the CEO of Google Israel, CEO of Meta Israel, the Minister of Interior Affairs, and the Head of the Israel Innovation Authority. Awarded 1st place and $50,000 in funding through the Google for Startups framework to further develop the project.
